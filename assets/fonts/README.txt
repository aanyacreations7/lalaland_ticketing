Brand fonts (Antique Olive / Antique Olive Compact, Linotype) are NOT stored in
this public repository: they are commercial fonts with a restricted licence.

If LaLa Land holds a web/webfont licence, upload these three files here:
  antique-olive-compact.woff   (headlines)
  antique-olive.woff           (body text)
  antique-olive-bold.woff      (bold body text)

The site detects them automatically. Without them it uses free look-alikes
(Archivo Black for headlines, Poppins for text) from Google Fonts.
